    Recovering developer capacity by reframing quarterly planning

    A survey said delivery was too slow and developers were in too many meetings. Following that led to a two-week innovation and planning iteration that consumed roughly 15% of the engineering year across more than 1,000 developers. Separating quarterly direction-setting from team-level delivery planning restored around 90% of story-point output during that window and returned an estimated 10% of annual capacity to project work.

    1,000+ developers ~90% output restored ~10% capacity returned ~$10m annualised value
